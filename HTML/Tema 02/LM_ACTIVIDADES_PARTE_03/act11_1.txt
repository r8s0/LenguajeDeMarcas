<h1>Quick hummus recipe</h1>

  <pre>This recipe makes quick, tasty hummus, with no messing. It has been adapted from a number of different recipes that I have read over the years.</pre>

  <pre>Hummus is a delicious thick paste used heavily in Greek and Middle Eastern dishes. It is very tasty with salad, grilled meats and pitta breads.</pre>

  <h2>Ingredients</h2>

  <pre>1 can (400g) of chick peas (garbanzo beans)</pre>
  <pre>175g of tahini</pre>
  <pre>6 sundried tomatoes</pre>
  <pre>Half a red pepper</pre>
  <pre>A pinch of cayenne pepper</pre>
  <pre>1 clove of garlic</pre>
  <pre>A dash of olive oil</pre>

  <h2>Instructions</h2>

  <pre>Remove the skin from the garlic, and chop coarsely</pre>
  <pre>Remove all the seeds and stalk from the pepper, and chop coarsely</pre>
  <pre>Add all the ingredients into a food processor</pre>
  <pre>Process all the ingredients into a paste.</pre>
  <pre>If you want a coarse "chunky" hummus, process it for a short time</pre>
  <pre>If you want a smooth hummus, process it for a longer time</pre>

  <pre>For a different flavour, you could try blending in a small measure of lemon and coriander, chili pepper, lime and chipotle, harissa and mint, or spinach and feta cheese. Experiment and see what works for you.</pre>

  <h2>Storage</h2>

  <pre>Refrigerate the finished hummus in a sealed container. You should be able to use it for about a week after you've made it. If it starts to become fizzy, you should definitely discard it.</pre>

  <pre>Hummus is suitable for freezing; you should thaw it and use it within a couple of months.</pre>
